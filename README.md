# Facebook Privacy Policy and Data Governance Analysis

## Overview
This project analyzes Facebook and Meta’s privacy policies, data governance frameworks, data collection practices, global privacy regulations, and major privacy-related controversies affecting digital platforms.

## Objective
The goal of this project is to:
- Understand Meta’s privacy and data governance practices
- Analyze user data collection and retention policies
- Evaluate GDPR, CCPA, COPPA, and CalOPPA compliance
- Examine data sharing and targeted advertising practices
- Investigate major privacy and security controversies involving Facebook
- Explore user privacy rights and digital transparency

## Topics Analyzed
- Meta Privacy Policies
- User Data Collection
- Data Retention Practices
- Cookies and Tracking
- Data Sharing with Partners
- Global Data Transfers
- Personalized Advertising
- User Privacy Controls
- GDPR and CCPA Compliance
- COPPA and Child Privacy Protection
- Cambridge Analytica Scandal

## Key Findings
- Meta collects extensive behavioral, device, and interaction-based user data.
- Personalized advertising heavily relies on activity tracking and partner integrations.
- GDPR and CCPA significantly impacted Meta’s transparency and compliance strategies.
- The Cambridge Analytica scandal highlighted weaknesses in third-party data access controls.
- Data governance and user privacy remain critical challenges for global digital platforms.

## Business Impact
This project demonstrates practical understanding of:
- Data governance frameworks
- Digital privacy regulations
- Compliance and risk management
- User data transparency
- Ethical implications of social media data collection
- Cybersecurity and information governance
